Every breach tells a story. Every log holds a clue.

Within The Logs investigates the hidden side of the internet: true stories of cybercrime, digital investigation, and the lingering traces left behind by hackers, insiders, and shadow operations.

mission.log

Within The Logs produces audio stories specifically intended to investigate, preserve, and explain the world of cybercrime, digital investigation, and the people behind the breaches, in order to educate and entertain both technical and non-technical audiences.

We adhere to journalistic standards through rigorous fact-checking and the ethical sourcing of information. We adopt the principles of truthfulness, accuracy, objectivity, impartiality, fairness, and public accountability.

The total service should be trustworthy, enhance intellectual development, expand knowledge, deepen aural enjoyment, increase awareness of what it means to live in a highly connected society, and result in a service to listeners that makes them more responsive, better-informed citizens of their digital lives.
